HOW TO PLAY

Drive your light cycle and leave a permanent trail behind you.

Crash into any trail or wall and you are eliminated.

Last racer alive wins.

CONTROLS

← ↑ ↓ →   Arrow keys to steer

⏎   Enter — new game

␣   Space — end game / restart

BUTTONS

↻   New game — restart from scratch

⏻   End game — stop and show scores

SCORING

+1 pt    per second alive

+100 pts   per kill *

−10 pts   on death  (min 0)

* only if they hit one of your last 10 placed trail cells

RACERS

You — human player

Reckless — aggressive chaser

Survivor — flees to open space

Balanced — mix of both

WIN CONDITION

Last racer standing wins.

If no one dies for 10 minutes the game ends as a draw.

The 10-minute clock resets every time someone is eliminated.
